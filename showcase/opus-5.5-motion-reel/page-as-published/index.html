How it's made

No stock footage, templates, plugins or sample packs. The pieces are code, so a new number, a new name or a new length is a re-render rather than a redraw.

  1. Storyboard and facts

    Each piece gets a beat sheet with timings, a sound plan and a source for every real figure before anything moves.

  2. Build as timelines

    GSAP timelines drive SVG, canvas and type. Every frame is a pure function of time, so any frame can be rendered on its own.

  3. Design the sound

    Every sound effect is synthesised with Web Audio and locked to the frame of the visual event it belongs to.

  4. Render

    Headless Chrome steps through the frames, FFmpeg encodes 1080p60 H.264, and the audio is rendered offline and muxed in.
